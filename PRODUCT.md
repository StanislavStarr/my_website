# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Potential clients of an independent developer: founders, small and mid-size business owners and product teams in Russia/CIS and abroad (RU and EN). They arrive from search, referrals or Telegram, and decide within a minute whether to write. Secondary audience: recruiters and tech leads checking credibility.

## Product Purpose

A personal business-card and lead-generation site for Stanislav Sinitsyn, an independent web and mobile developer (6 years). It presents who he is, selected team-project cases, services and process, and turns visits into inquiries through a short brief (email via Resend, or Telegram). Success: a visitor understands what he does and for whom, trusts him as a real person, and sends a brief. Also ranks for web/mobile development queries and is understood by AI search.

## Positioning

Direct work with one named developer, from design to launch, covering web, React Native/Expo mobile, PWA and Telegram Mini Apps, with SEO and AI-search readiness built in. No agency layer; specialists are brought in only when needed.

## Operating Context

Bilingual site (en default on `/`, ru on `/ru`) on Next.js + next-intl, deployed on Vercel (stanislavstarr.vercel.app). Cases open as intercepted-route modals. Contact: brief form (Resend), Telegram link, email.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Markets: RU/CIS plus international; two locales, hreflang, structured data (Person, WebSite, Service, WebPage).
- Cases are contributions inside team projects, not solo products; copy must keep saying so.
- No fabricated clients, testimonials, numbers or claims. Real material currently available: portrait and the existing case copy and metrics.
- Progressive enhancement: content visible without JS; motion respects reduced-motion and the on-site motion toggle.

## Brand Commitments

Retro game-console character with dark ground, amber accent and LCD-green panels, the snake game and the existing motion layer (curtain transition, scramble, magnetic and pill-fill buttons). The owner likes the current look and palette; evolve it, do not replace it.

## Evidence on Hand

Portrait (public/images), four case studies with roles and outcomes in src/content/projects.json, real contact channels. No client logos, testimonials, or extra photography yet; leave honest space for them rather than inventing.

## Product Principles

1. A real person, not an agency: voice and imagery must feel personal.
2. Prove with specifics from real cases; never invent proof.
3. One clear action: send a brief.
4. Findable by people and by machines (SEO, geo, AI search).
5. Motion adds life, never blocks content or access.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard operable, visible focus, reduced-motion respected, readable contrast on both dark and LCD surfaces; RU and EN parity.
